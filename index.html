About this laboratory

A manual-driven digital simulation

This site is built directly from the Food Chemistry Practical Manual (AS6201), Department of Applied Sciences, Mbeya University of Science and Technology. It is an educational digital simulation, not a substitute for supervised laboratory work — every hazard, procedure, formula and acceptance criterion shown is sourced from the manual, with any unavoidable gaps clearly marked as user-entered rather than presented as manual fact.

  • Vanilla HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript (ES modules) with Three.js for 3D laboratory glassware.
  • See docs/manual-mapping.md for a full feature → manual section map.
  • See docs/scientific-sources.md for the manual's own references.
  • See docs/asset-credits.md for 3D asset sources and licensing.
